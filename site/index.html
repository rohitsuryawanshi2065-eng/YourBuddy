Questions, answered

Is Nudge Buddy free?

Yes. Download it and use every feature, no account required.

Will it slow down my computer?

No. It’s a tiny window that only animates one small character. When nobody needs it, your buddy literally takes a nap.

Can it pop up over other apps?

Yes, that’s the whole point. It appears over your editor, browser, chat apps and even full-screen windows. It never takes over your keyboard, so it won’t interrupt your typing.

What happens during meetings?

Hit Pause from the menu bar / tray (30 min, 1 hour, or the rest of the day), or set quiet hours so it never nudges at night.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your computer. There are no servers and no analytics. Uploaded photos are resized and kept locally.

Can I get rid of the buddy but keep the reminders?

Sure: turn off “Live on my Dock” in Settings. Your buddy will only appear when a nudge is due.

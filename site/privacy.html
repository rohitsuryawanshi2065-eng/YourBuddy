Privacy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Nudge Buddy does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. Short version: everything stays on your computer.

What’s stored, and where

  • Your reminders, settings, streaks, display name and any images you upload as a custom buddy are stored locally on your device.
  • Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, servers, analytics or trackers in the app.

This website

This site is static. The “make you the buddy” photo demo runs entirely in your browser; your photo is never uploaded. Downloads are served from GitHub Releases.

Removing data

Uninstalling the app removes its data. You can also delete reminders and buddies, or reset stats, in the app at any time.
